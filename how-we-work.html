How We Work

No surprises. No fine print.

This page is the single source of truth for how ordering from BrownBarry works — MOQ, samples, dispatch, payment, COD, returns and GST billing. Everything we publish elsewhere links back here, so the terms never drift.

See the process

The Process

Four steps, from first message to delivered order.

  1. 1

    Browse & inquire

    Explore our ranges online, then message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your store, your city and what you sell. Our assistant routes you to the right person.

  2. 2

    Pricing & confirmation

    Receive honest wholesale pricing for your order size and category mix. Confirm designs, quantity and terms with a real team member — no bots for quotes.

  3. 3

    Dispatch with QC

    Your order is quality-checked and dispatched as quickly as availability and courier timelines allow, with a proper GST invoice and tracking.

  4. 4

    Deliver & support

    Pan-India delivery plus after-sales. Manufacturing defects reported within 7 days are reviewed for replacement or resolution.

Minimum Order

Trial MOQ 5 pieces. Mixed designs allowed.

We keep ordering deliberately flexible so retailers can test a range rather than committing to a single style in bulk. Mixed designs are allowed across our categories.

  • Order across designs — combine Kurti, Kurti Set, Suit Set and Co-ord Set in one order.
  • Choose from currently available kurtis, kurti sets and suit sets.
  • Mix designs to create a 5-piece trial order and test customer response.

Exact numbers confirmed in conversation — never forced on you.

Sample Policy

Samples are free. You pay shipping only.

We're confident enough in our quality to put a sample in your hands before you commit. The sample itself is not charged — you only pay the shipping or courier charges.

  • Available across all four categories: Kurti, Kurti Set, Suit Set, Co-ord Set.
  • One sample per design, subject to availability.
  • Shipping/courier is payable in advance; the garment cost is zero.
Get current set details

Dispatch

Fast, quality-checked, pan-India.

Dispatch timing depends on product availability and the courier partner's timeline. Our objective is to dispatch every order as quickly as possible while maintaining quality checks.

  • Every consignment is quality-checked before dispatch.
  • Tracking is shared once the order ships.
  • Pan-India coverage from our Mumbai base.
  • Courier charges are payable in advance.

Indicative dispatch timelines are confirmed at order time based on stock and courier.

Payment

COD, advance, or full payment — your choice.

  • Payment terms are written on your quotation before you pay.
  • Courier charges are payable in advance.
  • Full advance payment is accepted and may receive a 2% discount where applicable.
  • Eligibility for COD and the exact discount on advance are confirmed per order.

Returns

7-day manufacturing-defect review.

Manufacturing defects reported within 7 days of delivery are reviewed for replacement or resolution according to our policy.

  • Report defects on WhatsApp with photos within 7 days of delivery.
  • We review each case and resolve genuine manufacturing defects.
  • This does not cover change of mind, sizing assumptions made without sampling, or damage in transit handled by the courier.
Read full returns policy

GST Billing

Proper GST invoice on every order.

BrownBarry is a brand of Prime Apparel Exports, GST-registered in Mumbai. Every order ships with a genuine GST invoice, so you can claim input credit properly and keep your own books clean.

  • GST invoice included with every dispatch.
  • Entity: Prime Apparel Exports (Mumbai, Maharashtra).
  • MSME / Udyam registered.

Communication

AI-assisted first reply. Human for everything commercial.

Our WhatsApp assistant replies instantly around the clock to route you to the right person and answer factual questions (categories, sample policy, dispatch process, GST). For pricing, negotiation, stock and order confirmation, a real team member takes over.

  • Assistant available 24×7 for first response and routing.
  • Human team handles all commercial conversation.
  • Business hours: Monday–Saturday, 10am–6pm IST.
