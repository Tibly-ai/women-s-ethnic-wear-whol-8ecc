Why BrownBarry

You've been burned by suppliers before. Here's what we do differently.

We're not going to tell you we're the cheapest — we're not. We're going to show you, term by term, why retailers who value predictability buy from BrownBarry instead of taking another gamble.

The Problems We Built Against

Wholesale buying shouldn't feel like a gamble.

If you've bought wholesale ethnic wear before, you already know these. They're not bad luck — they're the standard experience in this category.

Advance paid, then silence

The money leaves your account and the replies stop. No dispatch date, no tracking, no one accountable.

Sample-then-switch

The sample looks great. The bulk delivery is a different fabric, a different finish, sometimes a different garment entirely.

No invoice, no recourse

Without a GST invoice you lose input credit — and any leverage when something arrives damaged or short.

Rigid single-style bulk

Forced to buy deep in one design you've never tested, instead of spreading a first order across a range.

Our Difference

Three things we do that most suppliers won't.

We're verifiable

A real registered entity — Prime Apparel Exports, GST & MSME registered, Mumbai office. Check us before you pay a rupee. We encourage it.

We publish our terms

Samples, MOQ, payment, dispatch, returns — written on this website before you commit, not negotiated after your advance clears.

We answer

An AI-assisted WhatsApp desk replies instantly at any hour, and a real team member owns your pricing, order, and after-sales. Disclosed honestly, because that's how we work.

Quality Standards

The sample you approve is the standard we ship to.

Every collection is designed in-house and produced under BrownBarry's QC — whether in our own manufacturing capability or at trusted partner units. Before dispatch, orders are checked so the bulk matches the sample standard you approved.

  • Fabric and stitching reviewed against the approved sample
  • Piece-level check before packing
  • Dispatch only after QC sign-off
  • 7-day manufacturing-defect review after delivery
