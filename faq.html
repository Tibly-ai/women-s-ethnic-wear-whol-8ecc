Samples

Sample availability, confirmed honestly.

Yes. The sample itself costs nothing — you only pay the shipping or courier charges. We can afford this policy because the bulk you order later matches the sample you approved.

That's the standard we QC against. Bulk orders are checked against the approved sample's fabric, stitching, and finish before dispatch. Sample-then-switch is the industry problem we built this company against.

Quality is controlled at the manufacturing stage, not patched afterwards: collections are developed in-house, production runs to the approved sample's specification, and every order passes a final quality check in Mumbai before packing. Defects that slip through are covered by our written returns policy.
