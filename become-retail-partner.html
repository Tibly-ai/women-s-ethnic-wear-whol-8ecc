Sample Process

Start with a sample, not a leap of faith.

Every new partner is encouraged to order a sample first. The sample is free — you only pay the shipping or courier charges — and the bulk you order later is QC-checked against that same standard.

  • Pick a design on WhatsApp — we confirm availability
  • Pay courier charges only; the sample itself is free
  • Check fabric, stitching, and finish in your own hands
  • Order bulk only if the sample earns it
