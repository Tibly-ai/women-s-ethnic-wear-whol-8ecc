Q: Are you a direct manufacturer or a trading agent?

We are 100% direct manufacturers under Prime Apparel Exports, located in Govandi, Mumbai. All cutting, stitching, and finishing are done in our factory unit with zero trader middleman commission.

Q: Can I order a sample unit first before placing a bulk order?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage boutique owners to test fabric quality and fitting first. You can order a sample unit — you only pay the courier shipping fee.

Q: Do you provide GST tax invoices for business accounting?

Yes, 100% of our orders are billed under GST (GSTIN: 27FLJPS0304G1Z5). You get full legal B2B tax invoice credit for your store accounting.

Q: What is the dispatch time and COD availability?

Stock, dispatch timing, courier charges and payment terms are confirmed in writing on the quotation before payment. Tracking is shared after courier pickup.

Q: What happens if there is a manufacturing defect in a parcel?

We maintain a 3-stage QC pass, but in the rare event of a defect, we offer an easy 7-day replacement policy upon parcel unboxing review.
