Terms of Business

Plain-language summary of how we work with our retail partners. Full legal terms apply at order confirmation.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

BrownBarry is a brand owned and operated by Prime Apparel Exports, a business registered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. We are GST-registered and MSME/Udyam-registered. These terms govern wholesale transactions between Prime Apparel Exports ("we", "us") and our retail partners ("you").

2. Orders & pricing

All orders are placed via WhatsApp or our enquiry channels. Pricing is quoted per order based on your category mix and order size, and is confirmed in writing before payment. Prices are not displayed publicly on the website. The final written quotation and confirmed order details control product codes, quantity, price, GST, payment, shipping, dispatch and defect handling for that order.

3. Minimum order quantity (MOQ)

Our current validation offer has a trial MOQ of 5 pieces, and mixed designs are allowed. Availability is confirmed before the order is accepted.

4. Samples

Samples are free — you pay only the shipping or courier charges, payable in advance. Samples are limited to one per design and subject to availability.

5. Payment

  • Payment terms are stated on the quotation and confirmed before payment.
  • Courier charges are payable in advance.
  • Full advance payment is accepted and may receive a 2% discount where applicable.
  • Eligibility for COD and exact discount terms are confirmed per order.

6. Dispatch & delivery

Dispatch depends on product availability and courier timelines; our objective is to dispatch as quickly as possible while maintaining quality checks. Every consignment is quality-checked and ships with a proper GST invoice. Delivery is pan-India from Mumbai.

7. Returns

Manufacturing defects reported within 7 days of delivery are reviewed for replacement or resolution. See our returns policy for full detail.

8. GST invoicing

Every order ships with a genuine GST invoice from Prime Apparel Exports. You may use this to claim input credit in accordance with applicable law.

9. Conduct

We reserve the right to decline service in cases of abuse, non-payment, fraudulent activity, or misuse of our catalogue, pricing or imagery. Our catalogue and product images are for genuine buyer evaluation and may not be redistributed or resold as another supplier's work.

10. Changes to terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The version applicable to your order is the one confirmed at the time of order placement.

11. Contact

Questions about these terms? Message us on WhatsApp or email contact@brownbarry.com.
