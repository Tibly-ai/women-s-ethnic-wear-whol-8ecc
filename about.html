About BrownBarry

A Women's Ethnic Wear Manufacturer in Mumbai Built on Trust.

BrownBarry is a brand of Prime Apparel Exports — a GST- and MSME-registered business in Mumbai, Maharashtra. We supply women's ethnic wear to retailers across India, and we compete on one thing: being the easiest, most trustworthy company you buy from.

Founder Story

It started with how retailers were being treated.

BrownBarry was founded by Shadab, who spent time on the buying side of this trade and kept seeing the same pattern: retailers paying advances and then chasing suppliers for weeks. Samples that looked nothing like the bulk delivery. No invoice, no input credit, no accountability when something went wrong.

None of that is a product problem — it is a professionalism problem. So instead of building another supplier that competes only on price, we built a company around the things retailers actually lose sleep over: registration you can verify, terms published before you pay, communication that doesn't go silent, and a sample in your hands before you commit.

Why We Exist

Mission and vision, in plain words.

Mission

Help Indian retailers source women's ethnic wear through a professional, transparent, and trustworthy wholesale experience.

Vision

To be the wholesale partner serious retailers rely on — not the cheapest, the most dependable.

Core Values

The rules we don't bend.

These aren't poster values. They decide how we price, how we communicate, and what we refuse to put on this website.

Honesty before marketing

No claim, badge, or testimonial appears on this site unless it is true and verifiable. If we haven't earned it yet, you won't see it here.

Transparency in terms

Sample policy, MOQ flexibility, payment options, dispatch model, and returns are published — before you pay anything, not after.

Communication that shows up

An assistant that replies instantly, and a real person who owns your account. Going silent after an advance is the one thing we will never do.

Long-term over one-time

We'd rather help you place the right small first order than push a big wrong one. Repeat partners are the whole business model.

Manufacturing Network

Designed by us. Made under our standards.

We're straightforward about how our products are made, because most suppliers aren't. BrownBarry designs and develops every collection in-house. Selected products are manufactured through our own manufacturing capability; the rest are produced by trusted manufacturing partners we work with closely — all under BrownBarry's quality standards and final QC.

  • In-house design and product development
  • Selected products manufactured in-house
  • Trusted partner units, never misrepresented as our own
  • Every dispatch quality-checked before it leaves Mumbai

Business Philosophy

We don't compete on price. We compete on predictability.

Somebody will always be cheaper. In wholesale ethnic wear, "cheaper" often comes bundled with opacity, delays, and disappearing acts. Our bet is simple: if we are the easiest supplier to deal with — clear terms, honest samples, proper invoices, reliable replies — retailers will build their business on us. That is why our prices are fair rather than lowest, why our policies are published rather than negotiated after the advance, and why we would rather lose an order than make a claim we can't keep.

Quality Commitment

What we check before your order ships.

Quality in this category isn't a certificate — it's a habit. Every order goes through the same checks, whether it's your first mixed carton or a repeat bulk order.

Fabric & stitching review

Fabric quality and stitch integrity are reviewed against the approved sample standard for the collection.

Piece-level QC at dispatch

Orders are checked before packing so what reaches your store matches what you confirmed on WhatsApp.

7-day defect review

Manufacturing defects reported within 7 days of delivery are reviewed for replacement or resolution. It's published policy, not a favour.

The Journey

From an observation to a working standard.

  1. The observation

    Years around the wholesale trade made the pattern impossible to ignore: the product was rarely the problem — the buying experience was.

  2. The foundation

    Prime Apparel Exports was established in Mumbai and registered under GST and MSME (Udyam) — the paperwork done first, so every claim could be verified from day one.

  3. The standard

    Commercial terms are kept clear: current trial MOQ, mixed-design ordering, GST billing, payment terms and dispatch details are confirmed before payment.

  4. Today

    BrownBarry launches with a focused catalogue — Kurti, Kurti Set, Suit Set, Co-ord Set — and grows collection by collection, retailer by retailer.
